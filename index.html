About NextBridge

A bridge between classrooms and careers.

NextBridge Institute of Professional Studies (Kiserian Campus) is based at Nannet Plaza, 1st Floor (Kwa Maji), Kiserian. We train students across ICT, media, business, accountancy, beauty & cosmetology, hospitality, engineering and plumbing — combining theory with hands-on studio and lab work so graduates walk into the job market with real, demonstrable skill.

Our Mission

To equip every student with practical, market-ready skills through mentorship and hands-on training.

Our Vision

Accreditation & Recognition

Who examines and recognises our programs.

Every course sits under a recognised examining body. Here's what each one actually does.

CDACC

The Curriculum Development, Assessment and Certification Council — Kenya's TVET body responsible for developing and certifying competency-based curricula.

KNEC

The Kenya National Examinations Council — conducts national examinations, including certificate and diploma-level TVET assessments.

KASNEB

The Kenya Accountants and Secretaries National Examinations Board — the examining body for CPA, ATD and related accountancy and finance qualifications.

ICM

The Institute of Commercial Management — an international awarding body for business, management and administrative qualifications.

Academic Pathway

How programs are structured.

Most schools follow the same three-tier structure, so you can start small and build up — or dive straight into a diploma.

1. Short Course

1–3 months, open entry. Fastest way to test a skill or add one specific capability (e.g. Cinematography Basics, Computer Packages).

2. Certificate

Usually 1 year or 3–9 months depending on school. Entry grades typically D Plain to D-. A recognised, employable qualification on its own.

3. Diploma

Typically 2 years. Higher entry grade (often C-), the most comprehensive qualification, and the strongest platform for further study or promotion.

Exact entry grades and durations vary by course — see each school's course list for specifics, or use the fee & program calculator on the Admissions page.
